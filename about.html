About us

Your local friends in China

A small local team making travel across China effortless for visitors from around the world.

Magic China is a small, local team based in China, helping foreign travellers explore the country the easy way. We grew up here, we know the shortcuts, and we speak your language.

From Zhangjiajie's floating peaks and Sichuan's giant pandas to Shanghai's neon skyline, we run small-group and private tours across the country — every one with an English-speaking guide, all tickets and door-to-door transfers included. No queues, no confusion, no language barrier.

Prefer something bespoke? We also design fully custom itineraries around your dates, pace and interests. Whatever you choose, our promise is the same: you show up, we handle the rest.
